## Introduction

This guide will provide assembly instructions for the `16mm-Feeder` and `24mm-Feeder` accessories for the LumenPnP. Upon completion, assembly should match the image below:

![](img/2u.webp)

## Tools (Excluding SMT) 
- Hex Driver (2mm)

## Jigs
- Feeder Loading Station

## Important Note
Assemblies for `16mm-Feeder` and `24mm-Feeder` are the same, however, It is important to note that the `feeder-frame`, `pick-window`, and `feeder-sticker` parts have both `16mm` and `24mm` varieties. Make sure you print the correct pieces for the accessory you intend to build.

## `peel-gear` Assembly

1. Place `peel-gear-top-2u` into `peel-pivot-2u`. Be sure to insert the side of `peel-gear-top-2u` with the divot into the side of `peel-pivot-2u` that is raised.

    ![](img/1-asm-1.webp)

2. Insert `M3x30mm-flat-head` through `peel-pivot-2u` and into the flat side of `peel-gear-top-2u` to secure.

    ![](img/1-asm-2.webp)

## `pick-window` Assembly

1. Insert `double-torsion-spring` into `pick-window`. Pull the wire of the spring over the plastic to secure. 

    ![](img/2-asm-1.webp)

## `16mm-Feeder` Assembly
1. Insert `n30-motor` into `16mm-frame`.
    
    ![](img/1-n30-insert-1.webp)

2. Ensure the cables are nestled between the motor and the wall of the print.

    ![](img/1-n30-insert-2.webp)
    ![](img/1-n30-insert-3.webp)

3. Secure `n30-motor` with `drive-motor-bracket`. Use 2x `m3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head` to keep `drive-motor-bracket` in place.

    ![](img/2-bracket-insert-1.webp)
    ![](img/2-bracket-insert-3.webp)

4. Ensure that the 2x `M3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head` are screwed in far enough to sit below the wall of the frame.

    ![](img/2-bracket-insert-4.webp)

5. Press fit `peel-worm-gear` onto `peel-motor`.

    ![](img/3-peel-worm-gear-1.webp)

6. Press fit `peel-motor` into `16mm-frame`.

    ![](img/3-peel-worm-gear-insert.webp)

7. Press fit `peel-motor-plug` to keep `peel-motor` in place.

    ![](img/3-peel-worm-gear-plug.webp)

8. Plug the cable from `peel-motor ` into `feeder-mobo-ott`.

    ![](img/4-mobo-1.webp)

9. Plug the cable from `n30-motor` into `feeder-mobo-ott`.

    ![](img/4-mobo-2.webp)

10. Ensure cabling is inside of `16mm-frame` and `feeder-mobo-ott` is flush against the frame.

    ![](img/5-flush-1.webp)

11. Loop cables from `n30-motor` to ensure they fit into `16mm-frame` while keeping `feeder-mobo-ott` flush against the frame.

    ![](img/6-wire-1.webp)

12. Insert 3x `M3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head` and secure `feeder-mobo-ott`.

    ![](img/6-mobo-screw.webp)

13. Place `peel-gear-bottom-2u` into the frame.

    ![](img/7-bottom-gear.webp)

17. Secure `peel-gear-bottom-2u` with 1x `M3x30mm-flat-head`. Ensure that the screw is not tightened down so much that the gear no longer rotates.

    ![](img/7-screw-insertion.webp)

18. Insert `feeder-compression-spring` to the cutout in the frame.

    ![](img/8-spring.webp)

19. Place `peel-gear` assembly on top of `feeder-compression-spring` and push down to compress it. Continue holding down the assembly to keep the spring compressed. 

    ![](img/8-assembly.webp)

20. While continuing to hold down the assembly to compress the spring, insert 1x `M3x30mm-flat-head` to secure the assembly. Avoid overtightening.

    ![](img/8-screw.webp)

21. Place `feeder-compression-spring` into `release-lever`. Compress the spring into `16mm-frame` below the cutout. Once compressed, slide the spring into the cutout in the frame. You may need to use a small tool to push the spring into place.

    ![](img/9-lever.webp)
    ![](img/9-spring-tool.webp)

22. Secure `release-lever` with `M3x30mm-flat-head`. Avoid overtightening.

    ![](img/10-lever-screw.webp)

23. Insert `16mm-Feeder` into `Feeder-Loading-Station`. Drive the motor forward until the flat part of the motor is facing up.

    ![](img/11-drive-motor.webp)

24. Place `drive-wheel` onto the motor and secure.

    ![](img/12-wheel.webp)

25. Place `drive-motor-cover` over `drive-wheel` and secure with 3x `M3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head`.

    ![](img/12-drive-motor-cover.webp)

26. Insert `pick-window` assembly by inserting the two legs of `double-torsion-spring` into the cutout of `16mm-frame`. Hold down while securing with `M3x30mm-flat-head`. Avoid overtightening.

    ![](img/13-pick-window.webp)

27. Apply `16mm-feeder-sticker`.

    ![](img/14-sticker.webp)
    
!!! success

    You have successfully assembled a 16mm Feeder.

# Introduction

This guide will provide assembly instructions for the Reel Holder accessory for the LumenPnP.

![](img/reel-holder.webp)

## Tools and Parts
- Hex Driver (2mm and 4mm)
- 25x `reel-holder-plate`
- 125x `M3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head`
- 25x `m3x16mm-flat-head`
- 25x `m5x16mm-hex-head`
- 25x `m5x35mm-hex-head`
- 4x `m5x10mm-socket-head`
- 5x `m5-tslot-nut`
- 25x `feeder-compression-spring`
- 2x `vslot-extrusion-20mmx20mmx600mm`
- 4x `rubber-foot`
- 3x `6x9-bag`

## Prints
- 25x `dynamic-frame`
- 25x `static-frame`
- 25x `retention-clip`
- 25x `knob-1u`
- 25x `knob-2u`
- 2x `reel-holder-leg`


## Jigs
- `reel-holder-plate-jig`

## Base Assembly

1. Insert 2x `vslot-extrusion-20mmx20mmx600mm` into `reel-holder-leg`

    ![](img/16.webp)

2. Secure a second `reel-holder-leg` to the other end of the extrusion

    ![](img/17.webp)

3. Insert `m5-tslot-nut` into rail and push behind hole in `reel-holder-leg`. Do this for each corner of the Reel Holder (4x `m5-tslot-nut` total)

    ![](img/19.webp)

    ![](img/20.webp)

4. Insert `m5x10mm-socket-head` into each corner of the Reel Holder (4x `m5x10mm-socket-head` total) Ensure it threads through `m5-tslot-nut` 

    ![](img/21.webp)

5. Add `rubber-foot` under each leg of the Reel Holder (4x `rubber-foot` total)

    ![](img/22.webp)

!!! success "Base Assembly Complete"
    You may now start assembling 25x `reel-holder-plate`. It is beneficial to have the Reel Holder Base nearby during plate assembly so you can attach plates onto the base as you complete them.

## Reel Holder Plate Assembly

1. Sand edges of `reel-holder-plate` with 600-grit sandpaper

    ![](img/1.webp)

2. Ensure there are no sharp points anywhere along the edges. Once the surface is smooth, wipe the sanded area with a paper towel

    ![](img/3.webp)

3. Insert `feeder-comp-spring` into the cutout on `dynamic-frame`

    ![](img/4.webp)

4. Align `retention-clip` so that spring sits at the start of the pill-shaped cutout on the print. Slide `retention-clip` up to compress the spring

    ![](img/6-copy.webp)

5. Load `static-frame` and assembled `dynamic-frame` into `reel-holder-plate-jig`

    ![](img/9.webp)

6. Place `reel-holder-plate` onto jig

    ![](img/10.webp)

7. Insert 5x `m3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head` to secure the prints. Please note that the hole circled in red does not get `m3x12mm-self-tapping-flat-hex-head` and another screw will be inserted the next step

    ![](img/13.webp)

8. Insert 1x `m3x16mm-flat-head` in the final hole above `retention-clip`

    ![](img/14.webp)

9. If the 1x `m3x16mm-flat-head` is fully tightened, the `retention-clip` will get stuck when compressed. The image below shows the `retention-clip` stuck in the compressed position because the screw is too tight

    ![](img/18.webp)

10. Slowly loosen `m3x16mm-flat-head` while testing the `retention-clip` until it functions as intended

!!! warning "Important Note"
    
    Each Reel Holder ships with 25x `reel-holder-plate`. Repeat steps 1-10 until you have 25 assembled plates.

## Knob Assembly

Knobs are often assembled in large batches, so there may be some that are already assembled and ready to go. They would be in marked white bins at the workbench.

![](img/31.webp)

If there are no knobs already assembled, prints will be in the boxes above the workbench.

![](img/30.webp)




1. For `1u-knob`, insert `m5x16mm-hex-head` into the stem of the knob print. Ensure the hex profile of the bolt is aligned with the hex profile of the print and push the bolt into place. Use a mallet to make flush if necessary.

    ![](img/32.webp)

2. For `2u-knob`, follow the same instructions but use `m5x35mm-hex-head`.

!!! warning "Important Note"
    
    Each Reel Holder ships with 25x `1u-knob` and 10x '2u-knob`. Repeat steps until you have the required amount of assembled knobs.

## Prepare for Packout

1. Attach 25x assembled `reel-holder-plate` onto the base

    ![](img/23.webp)

2. Add 25x `1u-knob` prints into a `6x9-bag`

    ![](img/29.webp)

3. Add 10x `2u-knob` prints into a `6x9-bag`

    ![](img/28.webp)

4. Bundle both assembled bags into a final `6x9-bag`

    ![](img/27.webp)

5. Place both the assembled Reel Holder and bag onto a cart to await packout

    ![](img/24.webp)

!!! success "All Done!"
    The Reel Holder is fully assembled and ready for packout.

## Assembly 
### Preping Container
*   Grab an air assist container and a pair of needle nose pliers. Remove the support under the solenoid clips. 

    <img src="img/Removing Clip Support 1_result.webp"/>

*   Be sure to grab the support as close a possible to the container clips and body to minimize left over support after removal.

    <img src="img/Removing Clip Support 2_result.webp"/>

    <img src="img/Removing Clip Support 3_result.webp"/>

*   Pick up one M3 square nut and insert it into the far corner of the air assist container.

    <img src="img/Inserting Square Nut 1_result.webp"/>

    <img src="img/Inserting Square Nut 2_result.webp"/>

*   Make sure visually that the nut is centered within the pocket.

    <img src="img/Inserting Square Nut 3_result.webp"/>


### Preping Solonoid
*   Slip a short section of tubing 20mm on the barb fitting that is ajacent to the metal one. Then, connect the right angle reducer fitting with the larger connector side to the other end of the tubing.
    
    <img src="img/Solonid Step 1_result.webp"/>

### Installing Solonoid
*   Put the reducer fitting loosly in the corner and press the solonoid into the clips with the only plastic barb exposed pointed at the wall of the container where the hole is. Push the solonoid until you hear a click.

    !!! danger "Guide the Wire"
        
        Be cognizant about tucking the wire of the solonoid towards the metal tube fitting and away from the walls of the solonoid mount to prevent pinching during assembly.

    <img src="img/Solonoid Step 2_result.webp"/>

*   Use an M3x22mm flat head screw to secure the right angle air fitting.

    <img src="img/Reducer Fitting 1_result.webp"/>
    
*   Guide the wire around the first cable clip.

    <img src="img/Solonoid Step 3_result.webp"/>

*   Guide the wire around the second cable clip.

    <img src="img/Solonoid Step 4_result.webp"/>

### Installing Air Pump

*   Strech a silicone rubber band over one of the vacuum motor posts.

    <img src="img/Motor Mount Step 1_result.webp"/>

*   Twist the rubber band and stretch it over to the other side 

    <img src="img/Motor Mount Step 2_result.webp"/>

*   Install a 16mm piece of tubing onto the center barb fitting of the vacuum motor.

    <img src="img/Motor Mount Step 3_result.webp"/>

*   Angle the vacuum pump and connect it to the metal barb of the solonoid valve. Then, lay it across the rubber bands.

    <img src="img/Motor Mount Step 4_result.webp"/>

*   Finish mounting the motor by connecting the free rubber bands accross the motor to their respective rubber band mounts.

    <img src="img/Motor Mount Step 5_result.webp"/>

### Assembling Lid
*   Pass the vacuum pump and solonoid valve wires through the control box lid

    <img src="img/Control Box Lid Step 2_result.webp"/>

*   Align the air assist container lip with the control box lid grove.

    <img src="img/Control Box Lid Step 3_result.webp"/>

*   Screw the air assist container to the lid with six M3x12 self tapping screws.

    <img src="img/Control Box Lid Step 4_result.webp"/>

    <img src="img/Control Box Lid Step 5_result.webp"/>

## Auger Tool Head
### Installing Square Nuts
*   Install two M3 square nuts into the auger stepper mount 3d print. You might have to use the blunt end of a pair of tweezers to push the nuts down.

    <img src="img/Auger Mount 1_result.webp"/>

    <img src="img/Auger Mount 2_result.webp"/>

### Ensuring Fitment and Tolerances
*   Tighten each luer lock connection on the auger componet to ensure a good seal that will last into the future

    <img src="img/Auger Mount 4_result.webp"/>

*   Install the luer lock extender onto the white right angle luer lock connector of the auger

    <img src="img/Auger Mount 6_result.webp"/>

*   Set the auger in the auger toolhead mount and cover it with the clamp. Ensure that the fitment is within reason. 

    <img src="img/Auger Mount 7_result.webp"/>

### Screw the Auger Mount Together
*   Start screwing eight M3x12mm self tapping screws in the auger clamp.

    <img src="img/Auger Mount 9_result.webp"/>
